User Guide

Overview

Maintenance Tracker is this hotel's shared job list — every problem raised, who logged it, and what's being done about it, all in one place, updated live on every phone. No more chasing "did anyone fix the light in 304 yet?" through the group chat.

Install it properly so it behaves like a real app: on Android (Chrome), menu → Add to Home screen; on iPhone (Safari), Share icon → Add to Home Screen. It then opens full-screen and keeps working even with no signal.

Your name and role always show at the top, just under the header — check it's you before logging anything.

Everyone (once signed in)

  • The main list groups every job by area/floor, then room.
  • Tap a job to see the full detail — who logged it, when, and the complete note history.
  • Use the search box to jump to a room number, or the status chips (Open / In Progress / Awaiting Parts / Done) to filter.
  • 🔔 shows what's changed since you last checked.
  • The clock icon opens Walk History — every Fire & Security Walk that's been done, grouped by day. Everyone can view this, including Management.

Housekeeping, Night Team & Duty Managers

Spotted something broken? Log it here instead of the group chat — it goes straight into the job list.

  • Tap the + button (bottom right) → Report a problem.
  • Pick the room, use Quick Pick if your issue's a common one (or describe it yourself), then submit.
  • It's automatically tagged with your department and lands in the list as Open — Maintenance picks it up from there. You'll get a 🔔 if its status changes.
  • You can't edit or delete a report once submitted — if you made a mistake, mention it to Maintenance directly.

Fire & Security Walk

This is where you actually write down what a walk found — the CoGard tag scan alone doesn't let you log notes.

  • Tap the clipboard-checklist icon in the header. Available to Maintenance and Housekeeping-role accounts, which covers night staff and duty managers too.
  • You're walked floor by floor, highest to lowest, then any other areas (Bar, Kitchen, etc.) after.
  • On each floor: tap any faults found — leave them all untapped if it's all in order — add a note if useful, then Next floor.
  • Nothing saves until you tap Finish walk on the last floor. Cancel walk at any point throws the whole thing away — nothing half-logged.
  • Every walk is recorded, even a fully clean one, so there's proof it happened — timestamped floor by floor.

Maintenance

  • Full control over every job: tap + to log a new one, tap any job to edit it, tap the status pill to cycle it Open → In Progress → Awaiting Parts → Done.
  • New jobs always start Open — there's no skipping ahead.
  • Notes are permanent once added — a running, timestamped thread that's never overwritten.
  • ⚙ Settings manages: site name, Areas (floors/zones), Common Issues (quick-pick shortcuts), Departments (who a job can be "Reported by"), Walk Faults (the walk checklist), and Rooms.
  • Only Maintenance can delete a job. Roles and PINs are set up separately via the Firebase console, not in the app.

Management

Read-only — a live dashboard of what's outstanding, nothing to fill in.

  • See the same job list and counts everyone else does, filtered and searched the same way.
  • No editing, no New Job button — pass anything that needs raising to Housekeeping or Maintenance.
