User Guide

Overview

Maintenance Tracker is this hotel's shared job list — every problem raised, who logged it, and what's being done about it, all in one place, updated live on every phone. No more chasing "did anyone fix the light in 304 yet?" through the group chat.

Install it properly so it behaves like a real app: on Android (Chrome), menu → Add to Home screen; on iPhone (Safari), Share icon → Add to Home Screen. It then opens full-screen and keeps working even with no signal.

Your name and role always show at the top, just under the header — check it's you before logging anything.

Signing in lands you on a Home screen of icon tiles linking into whatever this app covers for your role — tap the 🏠 icon in the header (or the header title) any time to get back to it.

Everyone (once signed in)

  • The main list groups every job by area/floor, then room. By default it shows everything except Done jobs, so it stays a list of what's actually outstanding — tap the All or Done chip to see completed jobs too.
  • Every area starts collapsed — tap its heading to open it. A number badge shows how many jobs are inside; it turns red if one of them is new. Opening that job clears its own red flag — no need to go through the 🔔 panel just to dismiss something you've already looked at.
  • Tap a job to see the full detail — who logged it, when, and the complete note history.
  • Use the search box to jump to a room number, or the status chips (Active / All / Open / In Progress / Awaiting Parts / Done) to filter.
  • 🔔 shows what's changed since you last checked, with a short beep when something genuinely new comes in while you have the app open (nothing plays for a backlog from while it was closed — only for things that arrive while you're using it).
  • The clock icon opens Walk History — every Fire & Security Walk that's been done, grouped by day. Everyone can view this, including Management.

Housekeeping, Night Team & Duty Managers

Spotted something broken? Log it here instead of the group chat — it goes straight into the job list.

  • Tap the + button (bottom right) → Report a problem.
  • Pick the area/floor first, then the room (this narrows the room list down to just that area), use Quick Pick if your issue's a common one (or describe it yourself), then submit.
  • It's automatically tagged with your department and lands in the list as Open — Maintenance picks it up from there. You'll get a 🔔 if its status changes.
  • You can't edit or delete a report once submitted — if you made a mistake, mention it to Maintenance directly.

Fire & Security Walk

This is where you actually write down what a walk found — the CoGard tag scan alone doesn't let you log notes.

  • Tap the clipboard-checklist icon in the header. Available to Maintenance and Housekeeping-role accounts, which covers night staff and duty managers too.
  • You're walked floor by floor, highest to lowest, then any other areas (Bar, Kitchen, etc.) after.
  • On each floor: tap any faults found — leave them all untapped if it's all in order. Tapping one reveals a room picker next to it, defaulting to that floor's general corridor room but switchable to the actual room a fault belongs to (e.g. an AC panel's P10 fault, or the nearest room to a corridor/emergency light) — each fault keeps its own room. Add a note if useful, then Next floor.
  • Nothing saves until you tap Finish walk on the last floor. Cancel walk at any point throws the whole thing away — nothing half-logged.
  • Every walk is recorded, even a fully clean one, so there's proof it happened — timestamped floor by floor.
  • A fault that's already an open job (still being sorted from an earlier walk) won't be logged again as a duplicate — it stays as the one job until it's marked Done, however many walks re-find it in the meantime. Check Walk History if you want to see exactly which days it was re-confirmed.

Maintenance

  • Full control over every job: tap + to log a new one, tap any job to edit it, tap the status pill to cycle it Open → In Progress → Awaiting Parts → Done.
  • Pick Area before Room when logging or editing a job — Room only lists that area's rooms, rather than every room in the hotel in one long list.
  • New jobs always start Open — there's no skipping ahead.
  • Notes are permanent once added — a running, timestamped thread that's never overwritten.
  • Marking Done needs a note saying what was actually done — tapping the pill straight to Done opens the job so you can add one (or it opens with one already there), then Save. Everything else still cycles in one tap.
  • Deleting a job needs a reason — typed into a small confirmation before it's removed. The job disappears from the list, but the reason is kept permanently, just not visible anywhere in the app yet.
  • ⚙ Settings manages: site name, Areas (floors/zones), Common Issues (quick-pick shortcuts), Departments (who a job can be "Reported by"), Walk Faults (the walk checklist), and Rooms.
  • Roles and PINs are set up separately via the Firebase console, not in the app.
  • PPM (Planned Preventative Maintenance) — the calendar tile on Home — is for recurring/compliance tasks (fire alarm tests, legionella checks, lift servicing…) as opposed to the reactive jobs everywhere else. Add a task with a room, how often it repeats, and its next due date; optionally note a contractor. Fixed repeats keep the original schedule even if a check runs late (e.g. always the 1st of the month); Rolling repeats count from whenever it's actually completed instead. When a task falls due, it automatically becomes an ordinary job (tagged PPM) — marking that job Done the normal way (note required, same as any other job) is what pushes the task on to its next due date. This only happens while someone's got the app open, so a task due while it's closed won't spawn its job until the next time someone opens it.

Management

Read-only — a live dashboard of what's outstanding, nothing to fill in.

  • See the same job list and counts everyone else does, filtered and searched the same way.
  • No editing, no New Job button — pass anything that needs raising to Housekeeping or Maintenance.
